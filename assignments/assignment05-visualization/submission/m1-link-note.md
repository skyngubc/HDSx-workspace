# M1 Link Note

Auditing the flawed plot taught me to use clear units, honest scales, and
descriptive claims. For my M1 project on opioid-related death rates across
Canadian provinces and territories, I will label crude rates per 100,000,
show the Canada-wide comparison consistently, and make any suppressed or
missing data visible rather than treating it as zero. Because the intended
audience is public-health decision makers, the figures and dashboard will
state that crude rates are not age-adjusted, recent counts may be incomplete
because investigations are delayed, and the surveillance comparisons cannot
explain causes or justify policy conclusions on their own.
