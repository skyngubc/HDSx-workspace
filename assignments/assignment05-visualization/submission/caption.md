# Figure Caption

Mean BMI by income group across NHANES cycles among adults ages 20-80 in the
prepared NHANES Health Equity classroom dataset. Lines show unweighted group
means; records missing age, BMI, or income group are excluded. The y-axis
begins at 20 kg/m², so differences may appear larger than on a zero-based
scale. Results are descriptive and do not support causal claims or
population-level estimates.
