# Provenance and Stewardship Note

This analysis uses the repository's cached `nhanes_equity_v6.csv` classroom snapshot, prepared from CDC/NCHS NHANES public-use data. It is an offline course asset used here to practice cohort definition, missingness reporting, and descriptive summaries; it was not freshly downloaded for this analysis.

The results describe an age-eligible, complete-case subset of this classroom file. The analysis is unweighted and should not be treated as causal or as representative population estimates without the appropriate survey design. The CSV contains row-level records, so I will not upload it or its row-level contents to external AI tools, attempt to identify or link individuals, or reuse it beyond the intended educational context without appropriate review.
